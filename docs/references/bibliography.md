# References and Further Reading

1. Thor I. Fossen, **Fossen's Marine Craft Model**. Compact 6-DOF kinematic and kinetic formulation, notation, transformations, and model properties. https://www.fossen.biz/html/marineCraftModel.html
2. Thor I. Fossen, **Handbook of Marine Craft Hydrodynamics and Motion Control**, 2nd ed., Wiley, 2021. Companion repository and lecture material: https://github.com/cybergalactic/FossenHandbook
3. MathWorks, **Using MATLAB Projects in Simulink**. Project organization, dependencies, source control, testing, and referenced projects. https://www.mathworks.com/help/simulink/projects-for-simulink-users.html
4. MathWorks, **Organizing Simulink files and folders in a project**, 10 June 2025. https://blogs.mathworks.com/simulink/2025/06/10/organizing-simulink-files-and-folders-in-a-project/
5. Blue Robotics, **Technical Reference**. Vehicle, thruster, and product technical references. https://bluerobotics.com/learn/technical-reference/
6. Blue Robotics, **Thruster User Guide**. Operational and safety guidance for representative underwater thrusters. https://bluerobotics.com/learn/thruster-usage-guide/

Supplier data is not a substitute for characterization of the installed vehicle. Record the exact product revision and access date used for each design value.
